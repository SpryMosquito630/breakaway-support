Privacy policy

Your training profile and practice history stay on your device. If your build offers hosted video analysis and you choose to use it, your selected clip is uploaded for processing by Google Cloud and sampled images may be sent to Google Gemini.

About this policy

This policy describes Breakaway, the football practice app developed by David Tian, and this support website. The standard public build keeps training on your device and does not offer hosted video analysis. Some TestFlight or later builds may offer an optional Analyze feature; the hosted-analysis sections apply only when you use that feature. The retention section also explains a difference in earlier TestFlight builds.

Information stored on your device

Breakaway saves the information you enter so it can personalize practice and show your progress. This can include training preferences, plans, custom drills, playlists, practice history, self-reported skill assessments, and progression records.

If you provide optional onboarding answers, these can also include your name, age range, club, goals, height, weight, and food or allergy preferences. These answers stay in the app’s local storage. Breakaway does not send these profile answers or training records to the hosted analysis service and does not provide cloud backup or sync for them.

Optional hosted video analysis

Hosted analysis is optional. When you explicitly choose to analyze a clip, Breakaway uploads the selected video to a Breakaway service running on Google Cloud. Clips are limited to 30 seconds and 25 MiB. Videos may reveal faces, bodies, surroundings, or other personal information. Choose only clips you have permission to upload, including permission from anyone shown. You can keep using local training without uploading a video.

The service uses MediaPipe to estimate body positions on Google Cloud. For technique review, it sends up to 40 downscaled still images from the video, with their timestamps and coaching instructions, to the Google Gemini API. The full video is not sent to Gemini. Feedback and scores are subjective coaching estimates; they are not validated measurements of speed or ability.

The original video is processed in an isolated temporary directory that is cleared when normal processing ends. Breakaway does not save uploaded videos in permanent video storage. See below for separate retention of results, request records, anonymous identity, and provider logs.

Anonymous identity and app verification

In builds with hosted analysis, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous identifier when the feature needs to authenticate. You do not need to enter your name, email address, or a password. The native Firebase SDK retains this identity across app launches. Breakaway uses it to authorize requests and enforce daily limits. Firebase App Check verifies the app using Apple App Attest; this is not an advertising identifier.

The service also processes operation identifiers, a hash of the uploaded request, request status, and hashed daily usage counters so retries can reuse a result and usage limits can be enforced. Hashes are used for these operational purposes; they do not make a video anonymous.

Hosted retention and Google services

  • Analysis results, including estimated body-joint positions, expire after 24 hours. The service denies access to expired results immediately.
  • Request hashes and status records expire after 48 hours. Hashed daily usage counters expire after seven days.
  • These records use Firestore expiry policies. Physical deletion is asynchronous and can occur after the expiry time.
  • Firebase anonymous identities are separate from these records. Automatic cleanup is configured for eligible anonymous accounts at any time after 30 days from creation; it is not an immediate deletion promise. Accounts linked to a sign-in method are not eligible for that anonymous-account cleanup.

Google Cloud, Firebase, and Gemini also process information needed to provide their services. Operational and network logs may include technical request information such as IP addresses, request times, and status codes. The Firestore expiry periods above do not set retention for Google's operational logs or Gemini's processing records. We do not promise a fixed deletion time for those provider records. See Google's privacy policy.

No advertising or tracking

Breakaway does not include advertising or integrate third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not sell or share your app data with advertisers or data brokers. No manual account registration or in-app purchase is required; optional hosted analysis uses the anonymous Firebase identity described above.

Notifications and device permissions

If you choose to enable reminders, Breakaway asks for notification permission and schedules notifications locally on your device. It does not register you for a remote push notification service. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings.

The standard public build uses bundled practice photos and demonstration videos and does not request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, or location. Builds with optional video analysis let you select a clip using the system video picker or choose Record & analyze to record a new video. If you choose recording, camera and microphone permissions may be requested by iOS for video capture. You can manage these permissions in iOS Settings. Selecting or recording a clip and choosing hosted analysis determine what is sent; Breakaway does not upload your entire library.

Your choices and retention

You can change your training preferences in the app. In the version being prepared for App Store release, a successful Settings → Reset all data clears your saved profile, training records, and reminders, including an on-device recovery copy of a repaired save. Resetting is permanent; the developer cannot restore local training records.

Appearance preferences remain after a reset. In earlier TestFlight builds, including build 3, resetting may also leave an on-device recovery copy of a repaired save. To remove the app’s local storage completely, delete the app and its data through iOS Settings. Device backups and restores are controlled by iOS and your Apple settings. Resetting local training data does not delete the server-side Firebase identity, hosted results, request records, usage counters, or provider logs, and does not reset daily hosted-analysis limits. Hosted records follow the separate retention periods above.

Children and younger players

Local training does not require an online account or transmit profile or practice records to the developer. Hosted analysis uploads the selected video and uses an anonymous identifier as described above. A parent or guardian should help a younger player decide whether to upload a video, ensure permission from people shown, manage device permissions, and remove local data. Please do not post a child’s personal information in public support issues.

Support, external links, and this website

If you choose to open a support issue, the information you post is visible publicly on GitHub and is used to respond to your request. Do not include personal or sensitive information. GitHub manages accounts, hosting, and issue data under its privacy statement.

This website does not add analytics, cookies, forms, or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request information, such as your IP address, under GitHub’s privacy statement. External websites opened from the app or this site have their own privacy practices.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app’s privacy practices change and revise the effective date above. For privacy questions or app support, contact the developer through GitHub Issues. Issues are public; keep your question general and omit personal information.
