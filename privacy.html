Privacy policy

Your practice belongs to you. The public release of Breakaway works without an account and keeps your training information on your device.

About this policy

This policy covers the public iOS release of Breakaway, a football practice app, and this support website. It explains how the developer handles information when you use them.

Information stored on your device

Breakaway saves the information you enter so it can personalize practice and show your progress. This can include training preferences, plans, custom drills, playlists, practice history, self-reported skill assessments, and progression records.

If you provide optional onboarding answers, these can also include your name, age range, club, goals, height, weight, and food or allergy preferences. These answers stay in the app’s local storage. Breakaway does not send this information to the developer or operate a cloud account or backup service.

No advertising or tracking

The public release does not include advertising or integrate third-party analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not sell or share your app data with advertisers or data brokers. No account registration or in-app purchase is required.

Notifications and device permissions

If you choose to enable reminders, Breakaway asks for notification permission and schedules notifications locally on your device. It does not register you for a remote push notification service. You can disable notifications in iOS Settings.

The public release uses bundled practice photos and demonstration videos. Its available features do not request access to your camera, microphone, photo library, or location.

Your choices and retention

You can change your training preferences in the app. Use Settings → Reset all data in Breakaway to clear active training records and reminders. Resetting is permanent; the developer cannot restore local training records.

Appearance preferences and an on-device recovery copy of a repaired save may remain after a reset. To remove the app’s local storage completely, delete the app and its data through iOS Settings. Device backups and restores are controlled by iOS and your Apple settings.

Children and younger players

Breakaway does not ask younger players to create an online account or transmit their profile or practice records to the developer. A parent or guardian can help manage device permissions and remove local data. Please do not post a child’s personal information in public support issues.

Support, external links, and this website

If you choose to open a support issue, the information you post is visible publicly on GitHub and is used to respond to your request. Do not include personal or sensitive information. GitHub manages accounts, hosting, and issue data under its privacy statement.

This website does not add analytics, cookies, forms, or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request information, such as your IP address, under GitHub’s privacy statement. External websites opened from the app or this site have their own privacy practices.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the public app’s privacy practices change and revise the effective date above. For privacy questions or app support, contact the developer through GitHub Issues. Issues are public; keep your question general and omit personal information.
