Get support on GitHub

Open a new issue to contact the developer. A free GitHub account is required to post. You can read existing issues without signing in.

Support issues are public. Share the app version, your iOS version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Leave out names, contact details, health information, photos, videos, and other personal information.

My training history is on this device

The public release stores your preferences and training records locally. There is no Breakaway account or cloud sync. Keep this in mind before resetting the app or removing it.

Practice reminders

Reminders are optional. Enable them in Breakaway and allow notifications when iOS asks. You can change notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.
